About our club

Technology is better together.

We are a student club for anyone curious about how technology works and what they can create with it.

What we do

Campus Tech Club brings students together to learn practical skills, share ideas, and work on small projects. You do not need previous experience to join.

Our meetups include web development workshops, robotics activities, and informal presentations of student projects. Members help each other learn at their own pace.
